Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know.

What is Arrow Puzzle?
Arrow Puzzle is a premium logic game where you tap and rotate arrows to connect pathways and clear the board. It's designed to be both relaxing and intellectually stimulating.
How do you play?
The goal is to align all arrows so they point to a valid target. Tap to select, rotate to aim, and connect the flow to win the level!
Is it free?
Yes, Arrow Puzzle is completely free to download and play. We offer optional in-app purchases for cosmetic items and hints.
Can I play offline?
Absolutely. The game is fully functional offline, making it perfect for commutes and flights.
Is it available on Android and iPhone?
Yes! Arrow Puzzle is available on both the Google Play Store for Android devices and the Apple App Store for iOS devices.
Can I play online without downloading?
Yes, we offer a lightweight web version. Simply click the "Play Online" button to start playing instantly in your browser.
How many levels are included?
The game currently features over 500 handcrafted levels, plus procedurally generated daily challenges.
Is the game suitable for kids?
Yes, it's rated for all ages. It's a great tool for developing spatial reasoning and logical thinking skills in children.
Does the game contain ads?
We respect our players. There are no forced banner or pop-up ads. You can choose to watch rewarded videos for extra hints if you wish.
